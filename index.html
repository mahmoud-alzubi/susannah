01 — About

An Irish-Arab voice for the stage.

Susannah Al Fraihat is a Dublin-based writer and emerging playwright of Irish and Jordanian heritage. Her work moves between two inheritances, finding the humour, ache, and quiet friction of lives lived between cultures.

Her debut play, Fruit for Thought, was one of six chosen from over 500 ideas submitted to a nationwide open call for Theatre for One: This Ireland, written under the mentorship of Emmet Kirwan. It was performed in a one-actor, one-audience-member booth on the plaza outside Cork Opera House, alongside new plays by Marina Carr, Stacey Gregg, Louise Lowe, Mark O’Rowe and Enda Walsh.

She is now an Axis Assemble artist at Axis Ballymun, expanding her Fruit series with Forbidden Fruit — four stars at Dublin Fringe 2025 — and one of ten writers commissioned for Ill-Defined as part of Fishamble’s Navigator Project on health inequality.

01

Forbidden Fruit

2025—In development

Scene + Heard Festival · TenderWRITE Big Plays, Dublin Fringe · Axis Assemble

The second play in Susannah’s Fruit series. Joe craves more from life after tasting the “exotic” fruit of his migrant neighbour, Aziz; the bond that follows — ripe with humour and tension — turns on cultural misunderstanding, microaggressions, and the stereotypes we outgrow, and the ones we don’t.

First staged at the Scene + Heard Festival in Smock Alley’s Main Space in February 2025, it returned that September as a 100-minute rehearsed reading in TenderWRITE Big Plays at Dublin Fringe, where Binge Fringe Magazine gave it four stars. It is now being expanded under the Axis Assemble programme at Axis Ballymun.

02

Ill-Defined

2026Commission

Fishamble: The New Play Company — The Navigator Project

One of ten short plays commissioned for the Navigator Project, Fishamble’s collaboration with health researchers Profs Joe Gallagher and Mark Ledwidge on how marginalisation causes health inequality — work spanning Ireland and Malawi.

Susannah’s was among six selected from more than 220 public submissions, joining commissions by Carys D Coburn, Hannah Khalil, Jade Jordan and Rosaleen McDonagh. First readings ran from December 2025 into January 2026.

03

Fruit for Thought

2024Debut

Theatre for One: This Ireland — Cork Opera House, Cork Midsummer Festival

Susannah’s debut, and the first of the Fruit plays. One of six short plays chosen from over 500 ideas submitted to a nationwide open call, each new writer paired with an established one — Susannah was mentored by Emmet Kirwan.

Performed by Sarah Agha in a purpose-built booth for one audience member at a time, on the plaza outside Cork Opera House in June 2024. Produced by Landmark Productions with Octopus Theatricals (New York) and Cork Midsummer Festival; directed by Eoghan Carrick and Grace Morgan.
